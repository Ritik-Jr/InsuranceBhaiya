“What insurances are legally required vs. strongly recommended for EU freelancers?”
Insurance Bhaiya Analysis

In Germany, health insurance is legally mandatory, and personal liability (Haftpflichtversicherung) is culturally and financially essential to prevent personal bankruptcy. As a freelancer, Marco's greatest single financial vulnerability is a long-term illness that stops billable hours. Learn how to protect your monthly baseline with our Disability Income Protection Calculator and review our deep dive in Long-Term vs. Short-Term Disability.

Key Vulnerabilities & Financial Exposures

Exposure 01

Unlimited personal liability if an accident injures someone or damages property

Exposure 02

Losing client income during extended illness without employer sick pay

Exposure 03

State healthcare system penalties for lapsing coverage

Recommended Risk-Transfer Blueprint

Strategy Step 01

Statutory (GKV) or Private (PKV) Health Insurance tailored for Selbstständige

Strategy Step 02

Private Liability Insurance (Privathaftpflicht) with at least €10M coverage limit (approx €5/mo)

Strategy Step 03

Occupational Disability Insurance (Berufsunfähigkeitsversicherung) to safeguard earnings

Target Budget Allocation
€280 - €480 / month (including mandatory health)

Can an insurance company drop you or cancel your policy after you file a claim?

In most jurisdictions, an insurer cannot immediately cancel your policy mid-term solely because you filed a single legitimate claim, unless there was proven fraud, material misrepresentation, or non-payment.
