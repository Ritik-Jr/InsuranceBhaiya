Case Study Analysis 🇨🇦 Canada Age 35 & 37 • Toronto, Canada

Sarah & David's Insurance Decision

Married couple with a 2-year-old toddler and a C$650,000 home mortgage.

“How do we protect our child's university education and family home without overspending?”
Insurance Bhaiya Analysis

Avoid expensive whole life policies pushed by commissioned brokers. A 20-year level term policy gives Sarah and David C$1,000,000 in coverage each for a fraction of the cost.

Key Vulnerabilities & Financial Exposures

Exposure 01

Loss of one income would force home sale or deplete emergency savings

Exposure 02

Future university tuition and childcare costs (approx C$18,000/year)

Exposure 03

Disability impacting the primary income earner before retirement

Recommended Risk-Transfer Blueprint

Strategy Step 01

20-Year Level Term Life Insurance: C$1M coverage on both parents

Strategy Step 02

Critical Illness and Long-Term Disability supplemental benefits

Strategy Step 03

Clear legal wills appointing guardians and naming contingent beneficiaries

Target Budget Allocation
C$65 - C$110 / month

Unbiased actuarial baseline without broker commissions, hidden fees, or agent sales upselling.
