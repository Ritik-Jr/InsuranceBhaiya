Frequently Asked Questions: Insurance Terminology Quiz

Expert mathematical answers, formula explanations, and related comparison guides.

What is the difference between a Deductible and an Excess?
They represent the exact same risk-sharing mechanism: Deductible is standard terminology in the US, Canada, and India, while Excess is standard in the UK and Australia. Master vocabulary in our Insurance Terminology Quiz.
What is an insurance premium?
The recurring scheduled fee (monthly or annually) paid by the policyholder to keep the risk transfer contract valid and active.
Why are beneficiary designations so important in life insurance?
Beneficiary designations legally supersede instructions in a last will and testament, ensuring life insurance death benefits pass directly to chosen loved ones without probate delays.
