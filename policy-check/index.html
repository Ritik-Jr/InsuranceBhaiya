What This Means

Conducting an annual 10-point policy check protects against passive auto-renewals:

  • Carriers Change Terms at Renewal: Deductibles, wind/hail rules, and roof valuation schedules (switching from replacement cost to ACV) are often modified in renewal notices.
  • Life Changes Demand Policy Updates: Remodeling a kitchen, purchasing fine jewelry, adding a driver, or starting a business can leave recent assets uninsured unless documented.

Frequently Asked Questions: Policy Review Checklist & Clauses

How to interpret your audit results, address coverage traps, and ask insurers the right questions.

What are the most common underinsurance traps uncovered by this audit?
The three most prevalent vulnerabilities are: carrying state-minimum auto liability (e.g. 25/50/25), lacking an inflation guard endorsement on home replacement cost, and failing to secure own-occupation disability protection. Calculate your life coverage in the Life Insurance Calculator and auto limits in the Car Insurance Calculator.
How often should I retake this 8-point policy audit?
We recommend taking this audit every two years, or whenever you experience a major life milestone (home purchase, marriage, childbirth, promotion, or starting a business). Read our checklist in How Often Should You Review Insurance?.
Does this health check tool transmit my answers to insurance companies?
Zero data is transmitted. All 8 diagnostic questions and vulnerability scoring run 100% client-side in your browser memory. We hold no consumer database. Review our complete Privacy Policy.
