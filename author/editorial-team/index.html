Editorial Standards & Review Process

Every guide, Q&A response, head-to-head comparison, and scenario published under the Insurance Bhaiya Editorial & Actuarial Team byline adheres to our strict editorial integrity framework:

1. Primary Source Citations

We do not cite insurance sales brochures. Every statutory right, time limitation, and policy clause is cross-referenced with State Insurance Commissioner codes, NAIC model regulations, and standard ISO policy contracts.

2. Actuarial Verification

All numerical examples and trade-offs are audited against actuarial tables, loss ratio benchmarks, and real-world claim settlement distributions.

3. Absolute Independence

We accept zero advertising revenue, broker commissions, or referral fees from insurance carriers, maintaining 100% consumer advocacy.
