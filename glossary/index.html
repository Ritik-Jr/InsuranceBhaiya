Deductible

The amount you agree to pay toward a covered loss or claim before the insurance company pays its share.

Property & Auto

Actual Cash Value (ACV)

A valuation method that calculates the replacement cost of an item MINUS depreciation for age and wear-and-tear.

Explore Breakdown & Example
Property & Auto

Replacement Cost Value (RCV)

A claim valuation method that pays the full current market cost to repair or replace damaged property with new items of like kind and quality, with zero deduction for depreciation.
