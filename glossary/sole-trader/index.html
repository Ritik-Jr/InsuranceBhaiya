Dictionary Entry Legal & Statutory

Sole Trader

An individual who runs their own business as a self-employed person and is not legally separate from it.

Actuarial & Contractual Mechanics

In the UK a sole trader keeps the profits after tax but carries unlimited personal liability (unlimited liability). You must register for Self Assessment if your gross trading income goes over the £1,000 trading allowance. Few insurance covers are compulsory, but public liability and employers liability matter in practice, as explained in do sole traders need insurance and our sole trader insurance guide.

Quick Knowledge Check
Which is true of a UK sole trader?
They have personal liability for the business
Correct. A sole trader and the business are the same legal person.
• They are a separate company
Incorrect. A company is a separate legal entity.
• They must hold public liability by law
Incorrect. It is voluntary, though often contractual.
