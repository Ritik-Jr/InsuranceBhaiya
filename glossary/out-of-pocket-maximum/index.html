Health & Medical Commonwealth: Annual Cost Cap

Out-of-Pocket Maximum

The legal upper limit on what you must spend on covered in-network medical care in a single policy year.

Plain-English Explanation

This is your ultimate financial backstop. Once your deductibles, copays, and coinsurance add up to this number, your insurer pays 100% of all eligible covered care for the rest of the year.

Practical Claim Example

Liam faces major cancer treatments totaling $250,000. Liam's plan has a $7,500 out-of-pocket maximum. Liam pays $7,500, and the insurer covers the remaining $242,500.

Why It Matters to You

This is the single most important number for preventing medical bankruptcy. When evaluating health insurance, always calculate if your emergency fund can cover this maximum.
