Understand Insurance Terms

Decode confusing insurance jargon into plain-English definitions with zero sales pressure.

Deductible (or Excess)

The fixed dollar amount you agree to pay toward a claim or loss before the insurer begins reimbursing its share.

View Definition →

Premium

The recurring subscription payment made to keep your insurance policy active and prevent coverage lapse.

View Definition →

Copayment (Copay)

A flat, pre-determined fee paid at the time of medical service, doctor consultation, or prescription fulfillment.

View Definition →

Coinsurance

The percentage split (e.g. 80/20) of covered costs you pay after meeting your annual deductible until the out-of-pocket max.

View Definition →

Out-of-Pocket Maximum

The statutory annual spending cap; once met, your health insurer pays 100% of all covered in-network medical costs.

View Definition →

Replacement Cost (RCV)

Full reimbursement to rebuild or replace lost items brand new without deduction for age or depreciation wear.

View Definition →

Frequently Asked Questions

Straightforward answers about our independent educational platform and financial models.

What is Insurance Bhaiya?

Insurance Bhaiya is an independent financial education platform providing actuarial calculators, plain-English guides, and unbiased policy comparisons for consumers in the US, UK, Canada, and Europe.

How do your insurance calculators work?

Our calculators use standard actuarial formulas (such as the DIME formula for life insurance and statutory coinsurance/copay algorithms for healthcare) to estimate your coverage requirements without inflated sales estimates.

Do you sell policies or earn commissions?

No. We never sell policies, take broker commissions, or collect your phone number for sales calls. We are 100% independent and educational.

How do you adapt to different countries?

We localize terminology (e.g. Deductible vs. Excess), statutory regulations (NAIC in the US, FCA in the UK, FCAC in Canada), and currency modeling so recommendations match your country.

Is this personalized financial advice?

No. All information, calculators, and comparisons are for general consumer education only. Always consult a licensed, fee-only financial planner for advice tailored to your personal circumstances.
