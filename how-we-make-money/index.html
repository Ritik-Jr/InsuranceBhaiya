How We Make Money (And How We Do Not)

Unconditional financial transparency: why we reject lead selling, affiliate kickbacks, and broker bounties to protect editorial independence.

💡 The Zero-Lead-Generation Model Insurance Bhaiya will never sell your contact information to insurance telemarketers, will never take commissions on policy purchases, and will never accept payment for positive editorial reviews.

1. The Toxic Economics of Online Insurance Portals

When you search for insurance advice online, nearly every top-ranking website follows the exact same business model:

  • They create calculators that require an email, phone number, and address to view the result.
  • They instantly sell that lead to a network of 5 to 8 competing insurance agents for $30 to $120 per lead.
  • Within seconds, your phone is inundated with high-pressure calls from commissioned salespeople.

This monetization model corrupts advice. Websites are incentivized to steer you toward policies with the highest lead bounties rather than the coverage that mathematically suits your family.

2. Our Negative List (What We Explicitly Refuse to Do)

  • ❌ Zero Lead Generation: We never collect or sell your contact information.
  • ❌ Zero Affiliate Links: We do not include tracked referral URLs that earn kickbacks when you purchase a policy.
  • ❌ Zero Pay-to-Play Rankings: No insurer can pay to be featured in our articles, calculators, or comparisons.
  • ❌ Zero Display Ad Clutter: We do not display invasive banner ads from insurance carriers.

3. How Insurance Bhaiya Is Funded

Insurance Bhaiya is developed and maintained as an independent educational initiative supported by an independent research endowment and educational technology grants dedicated to consumer financial protection.

In the future, we may explore non-conflicting funding models—such as licensing our anonymized, open-source actuarial calculation libraries to academic institutions and non-profit consumer advocacy organizations. Even if institutional licensing is established, our editorial board operates behind an inviolable firewall with zero ties to commercial insurance sales.
