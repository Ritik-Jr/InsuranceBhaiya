Frequently Asked Questions: Comprehensive vs. Collision Auto Insurance

Direct answers to trade-offs, actuarial differences, and decision criteria.

Does Comprehensive auto insurance cover damage if I hit a deer or animal on the road?
Yes. In the insurance industry, hitting an animal (deer, elk, stray cattle) is classified as a non-collision peril and is covered under Comprehensive insurance, which typically features lower deductibles than collision. Check rates with our Car Insurance Calculator.
Does Collision insurance cover hit-and-run vehicular accidents?
Yes. If your parked vehicle is struck by an unidentified driver, the damage is covered under Collision insurance (or Uninsured Motorist Property Damage where available). Review our case study on Rahul's Car Accident Scenario.
When should I drop physical damage coverage on an older vehicle?
If the total annual cost of collision and comprehensive premiums exceeds 10% of your car's fair market replacement value, dropping physical damage coverage is mathematically sound. Learn more in our Complete Car Insurance Guide.
