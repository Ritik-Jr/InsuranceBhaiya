The Bottom-Line Actuarial Recommendation

For 95% of families, Term Life offers the highest death benefit protection during high-debt and child-rearing years for 5x to 10x less cost (model your numbers with our Term Insurance Calculator). See our case study on Sarah and David's Family Life Coverage and review our guide on Is Life Insurance Payout Taxable?. Whole Life is primarily advantageous for high-net-worth estate tax planning or lifelong dependents requiring a permanent designated beneficiary.

Frequently Asked Questions: Term Life vs. Whole Life Insurance

Direct answers to trade-offs, actuarial differences, and decision criteria.

Why is whole life insurance 5 to 15 times more expensive than term insurance?
Whole life guarantees a death benefit payout whenever you pass away (a 100% statistical probability) and diverts substantial premium portions toward agent commissions, administrative overhead, and a guaranteed cash value reserve. Term life only covers claims during the active policy term (roughly 1% to 2% claim probability). Compare lifetime costs with our Term Insurance Calculator.
Can I convert a term policy into whole life later in life?
Yes, most quality level term policies feature a contractual conversion rider allowing conversion to a permanent policy without providing new evidence of health or medical exams. Learn more in our Guide to Insurance Riders.
Is "Buy Term and Invest the Difference" always the best financial strategy?
For over 95% of families, yes. Purchasing affordable term coverage and systematically investing the premium savings in low-cost index funds yields significantly higher liquid net worth. Calculate your required family protection with our Life Insurance Needs Calculator.

Can an insurance company drop you or cancel your policy after you file a claim?

In most jurisdictions, an insurer cannot immediately cancel your policy mid-term solely because you filed a single legitimate claim, unless there was proven fraud, material misrepresentation, or non-payment.
