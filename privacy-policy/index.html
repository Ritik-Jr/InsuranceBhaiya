Privacy Policy & Data Sovereignty

Our unwavering commitment to privacy: zero personal data collection, zero broker lead selling, no account requirements, and 100% client-side calculator execution.

🔒 The Zero-Data Privacy Guarantee Insurance Bhaiya was founded on the fundamental principle that consumers deserve impartial insurance intelligence without sacrificing their personal privacy. We do not collect, sell, lease, or distribute your personal information to insurance carriers, lead brokers, or marketing networks.

1. Core Privacy Architecture

Unlike traditional insurance lead-generation websites that gate information behind forms requiring your name, phone number, email address, and home address, Insurance Bhaiya requires zero personal registration. You can freely read every guide, utilize every actuarial calculator, and conduct risk assessments completely anonymously.

2. Client-Side Only Actuarial Processing

Every calculator on this platform—including the Life Insurance Needs Calculator, Car Liability Estimator, Term Rates Estimator, and 8-Point Policy Health Check—runs strictly on your device using client-side JavaScript.

  • When you adjust sliders for annual income, mortgage liabilities, or vehicle value, that data is processed solely in your browser's local memory.
  • Financial inputs and diagnostic answers are never transmitted across the internet to our servers or any third-party database.
  • Closing the browser tab immediately flushes all calculation inputs from active memory.

3. Information We Explicitly Do NOT Collect

We hold no personal databases. We intentionally do not collect:

  • Names, Physical Addresses, or Phone Numbers: We will never ask for your contact information to view coverage recommendations.
  • Government Identifiers: We never request Social Security Numbers (SSN), driver's license numbers, or tax IDs.
  • Financial or Banking Credentials: We do not collect credit cards, bank account numbers, or credit scores.
  • Medical or Health Records: We never collect prescription drug histories, medical exam outcomes, or health diagnoses.
  • Policy Credentials: We never ask for your insurance account credentials or policy documents.

4. Server Logs & Anonymous Network Telemetry

When accessing our web pages, our web server software and secure Content Delivery Network (CDN) automatically record standard, non-identifying technical request parameters. These ephemeral logs contain:

  • The requesting IP address (used for geo-distributed caching and DDoS mitigation).
  • The requested uniform resource identifier (URI) and timestamp.
  • Browser user-agent strings and HTTP referrer headers.

These technical logs are used strictly for infrastructure performance, error debugging, and security protection. They are never linked to personal identities and are routinely purged.

5. Cookies & Local Browser Storage

Insurance Bhaiya does not use third-party marketing or tracking cookies. We do not embed Facebook/Meta Pixels, TikTok Pixels, or Google Remarketing tags. We utilize minimal local storage solely to retain your client-side visual preferences (such as light/dark mode) and temporarily cache slider state so your session isn't lost if you refresh. For complete details, see our dedicated Cookie Policy.

6. Statutory Consumer Privacy Rights (CCPA / GDPR)

Under international and state privacy statutes, including the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA) and the European Union General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR):

  • Right to Know & Access: You have the right to know what personal data is collected. (Insurance Bhaiya collects zero personal data).
  • Right to Delete: Because we retain no user accounts or personal profiles, there is no personal record database to delete.
  • Do Not Sell My Personal Info: We do not sell, rent, or trade consumer data to third parties. We never have, and never will.
