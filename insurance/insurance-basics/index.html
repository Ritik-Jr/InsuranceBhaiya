Frequently Asked Questions About Insurance Basics

Common policy questions, coverage benchmarks, and companion decision tools.

Is term insurance really better than whole life for most people?
Yes. For the overwhelming majority of consumers, buying level term insurance and investing the premium difference in low-cost index funds yields vastly superior wealth and financial security. Read our in-depth analysis in Term vs. Whole Life Policy Comparison and estimate rates in the Coverage Calculator.
What happens if I outlive my 20-year or 30-year term policy?
If you outlive your term, the policy terminates with no residual value, having successfully fulfilled its role as pure risk protection. Most policies allow you to renew annually at higher rates or convert to permanent coverage without a medical exam. Learn more in What Happens When Insurance Expires.
Can I cancel a term insurance policy at any time?
Yes. Term insurance carries no surrender penalties or cancellation fees. You can discontinue premium payments at any time and coverage will lapse at the end of the 30-day grace period. Review our guide on What Happens If You Stop Paying Insurance?.

Can an insurance company drop you or cancel your policy after you file a claim?

In most jurisdictions, an insurer cannot immediately cancel your policy mid-term solely because you filed a single legitimate claim, unless there was proven fraud, material misrepresentation, or non-payment.
