Frequently Asked Questions About Life Insurance

Common policy questions, coverage benchmarks, and companion decision tools.

How much life insurance coverage does a typical family need?
Actuaries typically recommend 10 to 15 times gross annual income plus the outstanding mortgage balance and anticipated college education costs. Calculate your exact requirement with our Life Insurance Needs Calculator or the Insurance Needs (DIME) Calculator.
What is the fundamental difference between Term and Whole Life insurance?
Term life provides temporary pure death benefit protection for a fixed period (10-30 years) with low premiums. Whole life provides permanent lifetime protection and a cash value savings component, but costs 5 to 10 times more. See our financial evaluation in the Term vs. Whole Life Comparison.
What medical underwriting tests are required for life insurance?
Underwriters review your medical history, motor vehicle record, prescription drug database records, and typically administer a paramedical exam measuring blood pressure, blood glucose, cholesterol, and nicotine cotinine levels. Read our complete guide to What is Insurance Underwriting?.

Can an insurance company drop you or cancel your policy after you file a claim?

In most jurisdictions, an insurer cannot immediately cancel your policy mid-term solely because you filed a single legitimate claim, unless there was proven fraud, material misrepresentation, or non-payment.
