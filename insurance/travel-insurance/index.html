Frequently Asked Questions About Travel Insurance

Common policy questions, coverage benchmarks, and companion decision tools.

When is purchasing comprehensive travel insurance mathematically justified?
Travel insurance is essential when traveling internationally where your domestic health insurance does not provide out-of-country emergency medical or medical evacuation coverage, or when booking non-refundable vacations exceeding 5% of annual savings. Read our Complete Travel Insurance Guide.
Does travel medical insurance cover pre-existing health conditions?
Most standard travel policies exclude pre-existing conditions unless you purchase the policy within 14 to 21 days of making your initial trip deposit and qualify for a Pre-Existing Condition Exclusion Waiver. See our guide on What is an Insurance Exclusion?.
What does "Cancel For Any Reason" (CFAR) coverage provide?
CFAR is an optional endorsement that reimburses 50% to 75% of non-refundable trip expenses if you cancel for reasons not otherwise covered under standard trip cancellation terms. Learn more about endorsements in What is an Insurance Endorsement?.
