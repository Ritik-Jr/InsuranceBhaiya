Dairyland Insurance Phone Number

Quick Answer / Executive Summary

Dairyland Insurance's main customer service line is 800-334-0090, with a separate auto quote line at 888-344-4357 and a motorcycle quote line at 866-324-7952, per the company's own contact page. Dairyland is a legitimate, licensed non-standard auto insurer and a member of the Sentry Insurance Group, so it's worth confirming any number against the official dairylandinsurance.com site before calling, since search results also surface several unofficial "support guide" pages listing a different number. For the broader legitimacy framework this fits into, see [Insurance Company Reviews: Is [Brand] Insurance Legit?](/learn/insurance-company-reviews-is-brand-legit)

Key Takeaways at a Glance
  • Dairyland's official general customer service number, listed on its own contact page, is 800-334-0090; a separate quote line (888-344-4357 for auto, 866-324-7952 for motorcycle) is also published.
  • Dairyland is a real, established insurer — founded in 1953 by a University of Wisconsin–Madison professor and acquired by Sentry Insurance in 1966 — not an anonymous call center.
  • Sentry Insurance Group, Dairyland's parent, holds an A+ (Superior) rating from AM Best and reports more than $21 billion in assets, which is a strong financial backing for a non-standard auto specialist.
  • Dairyland specializes in non-standard auto and motorcycle insurance for drivers with tickets, accidents, DUIs, or lapses in coverage, and can file SR-22/FR-44 filings when needed.
  • Several third-party "phone number guide" websites list a different customer service number than Dairyland's own site; always verify against dairylandinsurance.com or your policy documents rather than a search-result snippet.

The numbers Dairyland itself publishes

According to Dairyland's own contact page, general customer service can be reached at 800-334-0090. Separately, the company lists 888-344-4357 for new auto quotes and 866-324-7952 for motorcycle quotes, with quote-line hours of Monday through Friday, 6 a.m. to 9 p.m., and Saturday, 7 a.m. to 6 p.m. Central time. Claims service is available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, 365 days a year, and suspected fraud can be reported separately at 877-736-8798. Existing policyholders can also manage a policy, make payments, and submit documents through the My Dairyland online account rather than calling.

Who actually answers: Dairyland's ownership and legitimacy

Dairyland Mutual Insurance Company of Wisconsin was founded in 1953, originally selling specialty coverage to drivers who couldn't get standard insurance due to poor driving records, limited experience, or no prior coverage. Sentry Insurance acquired Dairyland in 1966, when it was already writing non-standard auto policies in 27 states; today Dairyland operates as part of Sentry, a large mutual insurer with an A+ (Superior) AM Best rating and more than $21 billion in assets. That parent-company backing is meaningful context if you're calling Dairyland's number specifically to check whether the company itself is legitimate — it is a licensed, financially rated insurer, similar in category to NYCM or Erie, just focused on higher-risk drivers instead of standard ones. Sentry's own financial strength rating is worth checking too — see AM Best Rating for how to read one.

Why search results show conflicting phone numbers

A number of low-quality "phone number guide" websites reproduce Dairyland's customer service number inconsistently, and some list numbers that don't match the company's own published contact page. This is a common pattern with any well-known insurer's contact information — similar to the confusion search engines create around ambiguous quote sites discussed in our piece on "Insure 90" — and the safest fix is always the same: confirm the number against the company's own official website or the back of your insurance ID card rather than the first search result you see.
Real-Life Case Incident & Precedent
Precedent: Most states require an SR-22 or FR-44 certificate of financial responsibility to be filed directly by the insurer with the state's motor vehicle department after certain driving offenses, and only a licensed insurer can file one on a driver's behalf.

Case Study: Filing an SR-22 Request by Phone

Scenario: A driver with a recent DUI needs an SR-22 filing to reinstate their license and searches online for "Dairyland Insurance phone number," landing on a third-party guide site listing an unfamiliar number.

Resolution & Judicial Outcome: Before calling, the driver instead navigates to dairylandinsurance.com directly and confirms the official customer service number, 800-334-0090, along with the auto quote line. Calling that verified number, the driver reaches a licensed Dairyland representative who processes the auto policy and files the required SR-22 with the state at no additional fee, as Dairyland's non-standard auto program is designed to do.

What You Should Do: Step-by-Step Action Plan

1 Step 1: Get the number directly from dairylandinsurance.com/contact or the back of your Dairyland ID card, rather than a search-result snippet or unofficial guide site.
2 Step 2: Use 888-344-4357 for a new auto quote or 866-324-7952 for a motorcycle quote if you're shopping rather than servicing an existing policy.
3 Step 3: For an active claim, call the 24/7 claims line rather than general customer service to avoid being transferred.
4 Step 4: Set up a My Dairyland online account for routine tasks like payments and document requests, which can save you a phone call entirely.
5 Step 5: If you suspect a scam call claiming to be Dairyland, hang up and call 877-736-8798 to report suspected fraud directly.

Critical Mistakes to Avoid

  • Trusting the first phone number that appears in search results without cross-checking it against Dairyland's own official contact page.
  • Calling general customer service for an active claim instead of the dedicated 24/7 claims line, which can slow down urgent requests.
  • Assuming a non-standard auto insurer like Dairyland is less legitimate than a mainstream carrier — it's backed by Sentry, an A+ (Superior)-rated mutual insurer. See Non-Standard vs. Standard Auto Insurance Claims for how the claims process actually compares.
  • Giving personal or payment information to a caller claiming to be Dairyland without first verifying the callback number independently.

Can an insurance company drop you or cancel your policy after you file a claim?

In most jurisdictions, an insurer cannot immediately cancel your policy mid-term solely because you filed a single legitimate claim, unless there was proven fraud, material misrepresentation, or non-payment. However, they are legally permitted to non-renew your policy when the term expires (typically every 6 or 12 months) by providing advance written notice.

What happens legally and financially if you get into a car crash without insurance?

Driving without insurance and causing an accident results in catastrophic personal financial liability and severe legal penalties. You are personally liable for 100% of all medical bills, property damages, and legal judgments out of pocket. Legally, authorities can suspend your driver's license, impound your vehicle, impose thousands in fines, and mandate SR-22 high-risk insurance for years.

Why did my car insurance premium increase even though I had no accidents or tickets?

Car insurance rates can increase substantially without any tickets or accidents due to macroeconomic rate revisions, rising vehicle repair costs, increased frequency of catastrophic weather losses in your zip code, insurance credit score changes, and widespread insurance industry underwriting adjustments across your state.

Does your personal auto insurance cover rental cars, or do you need rental counter coverage?

In the United States and Canada, your personal auto insurance coverage (comprehensive, collision, and liability) typically transfers directly to a rental car driven for personal pleasure, up to your existing policy limits and deductibles. However, personal policies almost never cover 'loss of use' administrative fees charged by rental companies, and do not provide coverage internationally.
