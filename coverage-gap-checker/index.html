What This Means

A classification of Worth Reviewing does not mean your policy is defective or that you made a mistake:

  • Missing Information Gaps: Often, policy documents omit key endorsements (like water backup or true own-occupation definitions) unless specifically requested by the policyholder.
  • Inflation Drift: Construction materials and medical costs rise 4–7% annually. A limit set 5 years ago may have experienced significant purchasing power erosion.
  • Opportunity to Save or Shield: Reviewing these items with your licensed carrier allows you to plug vulnerabilities before a claim occurs.

Frequently Asked Questions: Coverage Gap Checker

Expert mathematical answers, formula explanations, and related comparison guides.

What is an insurance coverage gap?
A coverage gap occurs when an unexpected loss or civil lawsuit exceeds your policy limit, falls below an unaffordable deductible, or involves an excluded peril. Audit your policy with our Coverage Gap Checker.
How do inflation and rising rebuild costs create insurance gaps?
Building material and medical costs rise faster than general inflation. A home policy with limits set 5 years ago may cover only 70% of actual reconstruction costs today. Read our 5 Warning Signs of Underinsurance.
Does a policy check mean my current insurance is invalid?
No. A diagnostic check highlights items marked worth reviewing so you can verify exact endorsement codes and definitions on your declarations page.

Can an insurance company drop you or cancel your policy after you file a claim?

In most jurisdictions, an insurer cannot immediately cancel your policy mid-term solely because you filed a single legitimate claim, unless there was proven fraud, material misrepresentation, or non-payment.
