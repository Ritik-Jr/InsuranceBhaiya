Car Insurance Coverage Checklist & Analysis

Profile-Optimized Checklist
Essential Protection • High Risk

1. Bodily Injury & Property Damage Liability

Why It Matters:

Liability pays for other people's emergency room bills, long-term disability, and vehicle repairs if you cause a crash. State statutory minimums (e.g. $25k property damage) are easily exhausted by a single modern SUV or multi-vehicle pileup, leaving you personally exposed to wage garnishment and asset liens.

Tailored Profile Insight:
With estimated personal assets of $50,000–$250,000, carrying state minimum liability creates catastrophic exposure. You may want to consider split limits of at least 100/300/100 ($100k per person / $300k per accident / $100k property damage) or higher.
Questions to Review with Insurer:
  • What are my current policy's Bodily Injury and Property Damage split limits?
  • How much does it cost to raise Property Damage liability from $25,000 to $100,000? (Often less than $4–$8/month).
  • Does my total household net worth justify an additional $1,000,000 Personal Umbrella policy?
Contractually Mandated by Lender

2. Collision Coverage

Why It Matters:

Collision pays to repair or replace your vehicle after impacting another car, guardrail, ditch, or tree, minus your chosen deductible, regardless of who was at fault.

Tailored Profile Insight:
Because your vehicle is financed, your lender contractually requires collision coverage to protect their loan collateral until the lien is completely satisfied.
Questions to Review with Insurer:
  • What is my current collision deductible ($500 vs. $1,000)?
  • Does my lender contract restrict my maximum deductible to $500 or $1,000?
  • Compare how changing your collision deductible affects your annual premium in our Car Deductible Calculator.
Contractually Mandated by Lender

3. Comprehensive (Other Than Collision) Coverage

Why It Matters:

Covers vehicle damage from non-collision perils: animal strikes (such as hitting a deer), auto theft, vandalism, fire, hail, fallen branches, and windshield glass damage.

Tailored Profile Insight:
Comprehensive is usually substantially less expensive than collision, yet provides essential catastrophic protection against severe weather, theft, and animal strikes.
Questions to Review with Insurer:
  • Does my policy offer a zero-deductible or low-deductible glass / windshield waiver?
  • Is comprehensive coverage bundled with collision or can deductibles be chosen independently?
Strongly Advised • Defense Layer

4. Uninsured & Underinsured Motorist (UM / UIM)

Why It Matters:

Approximately 1 in 7 drivers on US roads carries zero insurance, and millions more carry only meager state minimums. If an uninsured driver hits you, or you are victimized in a hit-and-run, UM/UIM steps in to pay your medical bills, lost wages, and pain & suffering.

Tailored Profile Insight:
Actuaries strongly recommend matching your UM/UIM limits to your primary Bodily Injury Liability limits (e.g. 100k/300k).
Questions to Review with Insurer:
  • Do my UM/UIM bodily injury limits match my own liability limits?
  • Does my state permit stacking UM/UIM coverage across multiple household vehicles?
Jurisdiction & Health Dependent

5. Medical Payments (MedPay) / Personal Injury Protection (PIP)

Why It Matters:

Pays immediate medical expenses, funeral costs, and in no-fault states (PIP), lost wages and essential childcare services for you and your passengers regardless of fault.

Tailored Profile Insight:
In at-fault states, MedPay provides quick cash flow to cover health insurance deductibles and copays without waiting for fault disputes to resolve.
Questions to Review with Insurer:
  • Does my state mandate Personal Injury Protection (PIP) by statute?
  • How does MedPay coordinate with my family's private health insurance plan deductibles?
Discretionary Endorsements

6. GAP, Rental Reimbursement & Roadside Assistance

Why It Matters:

These targeted endorsements protect daily liquidity: GAP pays the difference between your car's market value and loan balance if totaled; Rental Reimbursement covers loaner cars during repairs; Roadside pays for towing and lockouts.

Tailored Profile Insight:
Because your vehicle is financed, GAP Insurance is highly relevant if you put down less than 20% cash, preventing you from paying thousands out-of-pocket on a totaled car loan.
Questions to Review with Insurer:
  • Do I already have roadside assistance through my vehicle manufacturer warranty or credit card?
  • What is the daily and maximum limit on Rental Car Reimbursement ($30/day vs $50/day)?
  • Is GAP insurance cheaper through my auto insurer than through the car dealership? (Usually 60%–80% cheaper).

What This Means

This assessment suggests that automotive insurance is not a monolithic product, but rather a modular series of financial defense layers:

  • High-Severity Risks (Catastrophic Defense): Bodily Injury and Property Damage liability protect you from financial ruin and lawsuits. Never economize on liability caps to save a few dollars.
  • Capped Property Risks: Collision and Comprehensive are capped strictly at your vehicle's Actual Cash Value (ACV). For older cars with minimal market value, physical damage insurance may cost more in premiums than any net insurance payout.
  • First-Party Defense: Uninsured Motorist and MedPay protect you directly against negligent drivers who disregard financial responsibility laws.

Actual insurance coverage depends on your policy and insurer. Always verify specific limits and local statutory mandates with a licensed professional.

Important Assumptions

Actual Cash Value (ACV) Rule

Insurers pay for vehicle damage based on depreciated cash value at the time of loss, never the original purchase price.

Personal Use Classification

Assumes standard personal commuter use. Commercial delivery or commercial rideshare driving requires dedicated commercial endorsements.

Standard Named Insureds

Assumes all licensed household residents of driving age are formally listed on the policy declarations page.

Tort Liability Statutes

State minimums vary widely (e.g., California 15/30/5 vs Maine 50/100/25); all statutory minimums leave significant civil exposure.

Things You May Want to Review

Actionable items to check on your current policy declarations page:

1
Policy Declarations Page:

Pull your current 1-page summary to verify your exact bodily injury limits and physical damage deductibles.

2
Financing Contract Deductible Caps:

Verify if your auto loan lender restricts comprehensive/collision deductibles to a maximum of $500 or $1,000.

3
Teen Driver Discounts:

If you have young drivers, ask your carrier about Good Student (GPA 3.0+) and Telematics Safe Driver discounts.

4
Multi-Policy Bundling:

Combining your auto policy with homeowners, condo, or renters insurance typically yields 10% to 25% across-the-board discounts.

Frequently Asked Questions: Car Insurance Coverage Calculator

Expert mathematical answers, formula explanations, and related comparison guides.

What auto liability limits should I carry for adequate protection?
State statutory minimums (such as 25/50/25) are dangerously inadequate against modern vehicular lawsuits. Actuaries recommend at least 100/300/100 or 250/500/100 limits paired with a personal umbrella policy. Read our deep dive in the Complete Car Insurance Guide and see our case study on Rahul's At-Fault Collision Scenario.
When does it make sense to drop Collision and Comprehensive coverage?
If your vehicle's actual cash value (ACV) drops below 10 times your annual physical damage premium, paying for collision coverage becomes mathematically inefficient. Compare coverage trade-offs in our Collision vs Comprehensive Comparison.
How much can I save by increasing my collision deductible?
Increasing your collision deductible from $500 to $1,000 typically reduces collision premium by 12% to 20%. Calculate how many claim-free driving years you need to profit using our Car Insurance Deductible Calculator.

Can an insurance company drop you or cancel your policy after you file a claim?

In most jurisdictions, an insurer cannot immediately cancel your policy mid-term solely because you filed a single legitimate claim, unless there was proven fraud, material misrepresentation, or non-payment.
