Your Travel Insurance Review Checklist

Non-Advisory Checklist

What This Means

This checklist suggests how to evaluate whether third-party travel insurance is necessary or whether existing coverage is sufficient:

  • High-Severity Risk is Medical Evacuation: While lost baggage is an annoyance ($1,000 loss), an emergency airlift from an ocean cruise or remote mountain back to an advanced hospital easily costs $100,000 to $250,000. Prioritize medical evacuation over minor inconvenience coverage.
  • Credit Cards Often Cover Cancellation: If you booked non-refundable flights with a premium credit card, you likely already have $10,000 to $20,000 in trip cancellation protection for covered medical reasons. Check your card benefits guide first.
  • Pre-Existing Condition Timing Rules: Most travel policies exclude pre-existing medical conditions unless you purchase the policy within 14 to 21 days of making your initial trip deposit.

Actual insurance coverage depends on your policy and insurer. This checklist is for educational review only and does not constitute a recommendation to purchase insurance.

Important Assumptions

Covered Reasons Only

Standard trip cancellation only pays for named perils (severe illness, physician-ordered cancellation, weather grounding).

Cancel For Any Reason (CFAR)

Canceling due to fear or scheduling conflicts requires an optional CFAR rider, which reimburses 50% to 75% of costs.

Secondary vs Primary Medical

Many cheap travel policies are secondary payers, requiring you to file with your domestic insurer first before paying.

Look-Back Period

Insurers review medical records for 60 to 180 days prior to purchase to identify pre-existing conditions.

4

If pre-existing conditions exist, confirm you are within the 14 to 21 day window from your first trip booking deposit.

Frequently Asked Questions: Travel Insurance Coverage Checklist

Expert mathematical answers, formula explanations, and related comparison guides.

Does domestic US health insurance or Medicare cover emergency care abroad?
Standard Medicare and most domestic HMO/PPO plans provide zero coverage outside the United States. Emergency medical evacuation from remote destinations or cruise ships can exceed $100,000. Audit your risks with our Travel Insurance Coverage Checklist.
When should I rely on credit card travel insurance instead of buying a policy?
If you paid for non-refundable travel with a premium credit card (e.g. Chase Sapphire Reserve or Amex Platinum), you likely already have $10,000+ in trip cancellation and primary auto rental collision damage coverage. Read our Complete Travel Insurance Guide.
What is Cancel For Any Reason (CFAR) coverage?
Standard travel insurance only covers named perils like sudden medical illness or severe weather. CFAR is an optional add-on that allows you to cancel for subjective reasons (such as fear of travel) and recover 50% to 75% of prepaid non-refundable costs.

Can an insurance company drop you or cancel your policy after you file a claim?

In most jurisdictions, an insurer cannot immediately cancel your policy mid-term solely because you filed a single legitimate claim, unless there was proven fraud, material misrepresentation, or non-payment.
