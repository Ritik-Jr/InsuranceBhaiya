How to Know If You Are Underinsured: 5 Dangerous Warning Signs

Identify the 5 tell-tale diagnostic red flags of underinsurance: building cost inflation gaps, state minimum auto traps, inadequate income replacement, and liability exposure.

IB
Elena Rostova
Actuarial Research Fellow • Fact-Checked

Key Editorial Takeaways

  • Review your current declarations page to confirm exact deductibles, sub-limits, and named insured designations.
  • Policy exclusions supersede marketing summaries—always verify specific peril exceptions in Section I and II.
  • Adjust coverage whenever life circumstances change (e.g., mortgage refinancing, marriage, or acquiring high-value assets).

Underinsurance is one of the most widespread and dangerous financial vulnerabilities in modern households. Over 60% of homes in the United States are underinsured by an average of 20%, according to studies by the Swiss Re Institute.

The 5 Red Flags That You Are Underinsured

The Coinsurance Penalty Trap

Most commercial and home policies contain an '80% Coinsurance Clause.' If you insure your home for less than 80% of its true replacement cost, the insurer will penalize you on EVERY partial claim, reimbursing only a fraction of the damage.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I find the true replacement cost of my house?
Do not use your real estate market value or property tax assessment. Request an itemized replacement cost valuation (RCV) from your insurance agent, which calculates square footage multiplied by local contractor building rates.
